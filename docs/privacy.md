# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30.

Palwyn has no servers and no accounts, and it collects nothing. The developer receives no data from the app: no analytics, crash reports, advertising IDs or usage statistics.

## What moves, and where

Palwyn connects your phone to the PC you pair it with, directly, over your local network, a USB cable, or an address you set yourself (such as a VPN). Everything travels encrypted (TLS 1.3, both devices pinned to each other since pairing) and only between those two devices. Depending on the features you allow on the phone, this can include calls and call history, text messages, contacts, notifications, photos and videos, files, the clipboard, and the phone's screen.

## What is stored

- **On the PC:** the paired phones and settings; the phone's last 500 notifications, if notification history is on (on by default, can be turned off and cleared); files and photos you choose to save; and logs kept for 7 days, which mask phone numbers and never contain message, notification or clipboard content. Call history, messages and contacts are not stored: the PC asks the phone each time.
- **On the phone:** the paired PCs, settings, and files the PC sends you (in Download/Palwyn).

Removing a paired device, or uninstalling the app, deletes what it stored about that device. Details per feature are in [security.md](security.md).

## Permissions

Each Android permission is asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs it, with the reason shown. You can refuse any of them and still use the rest, and revoke them in Android's settings at any time.

## Third parties

None inside the apps. The [website](https://palwyn.vercel.app) is hosted by Vercel and loads nothing from third parties until you open the Ko-fi support panel, which is served by Ko-fi. Downloads come from GitHub.
