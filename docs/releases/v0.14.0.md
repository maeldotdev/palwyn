First public pre-release of Palwyn.

**Install:** see the [installation guide](https://github.com/maeldotdev/palwyn/blob/main/docs/install.md). Download both files below: the Windows zip for your PC and the APK for your phone. Check them against `SHA256SUMS.txt` if you like.

**What's in it:** calls, SMS and MMS, notifications with replies and history, photos, Quick Drop for files, folders and links, clipboard, contacts, phone screen on the PC, the phone as a remote, and more. The full list is in the [README](https://github.com/maeldotdev/palwyn#features).

**Good to know**
- This is a pre-release: expect rough edges and please report them in [Issues](https://github.com/maeldotdev/palwyn/issues).
- Windows: the installer asks you to trust Palwyn's own signing certificate once. When signing through the SignPath Foundation is approved, that release will install as a new app on Windows, and you'll pair your phone again, once.
- Android: sideloaded from GitHub, so Play Protect may warn you. Future Android updates install over this one and keep your data.
- Call audio stays on the phone, and SMS only (no RCS): see [known limits](https://github.com/maeldotdev/palwyn#known-limits).
