# Name and logo

The code is free software under the GPL-3.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The name **Palwyn** and the Palwyn logo are not: the licence gives rights to the code, not to the name.

This keeps people safe: when someone downloads "Palwyn", it should be the real app, not a changed copy that could do anything with their messages and files.

## Fine without asking

- Saying that your project works with Palwyn, is based on Palwyn, or is a fork of Palwyn.
- Writing about Palwyn, reviewing it, or linking to it.
- Building the unchanged source code for your own use.

## Not allowed

- Publishing a changed version under the name Palwyn or a confusingly similar name, or with the Palwyn logo. Give your version its own name and icon.
- Suggesting that your version is official, or made or approved by Mael, when it isn't.
- Using the name or logo for a different product.

If you're unsure, open an [issue](https://github.com/maeldotdev/palwyn/issues) and ask.
