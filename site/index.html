One link. Yours.

Your phone and PC talk to each other directly. No Palwyn server, no account, no copy of your messages sitting in someone's cloud.

  • Encrypted both waysTLS 1.3 with pinned certificates. Only the devices you paired can connect.
  • Stays on your networkNothing leaves your Wi-Fi unless you point it at your own VPN.
  • Open sourceGPL-3.0. Read every line that touches your phone.

What it won't do. Honestly.

Android's rules

Phone to PC clipboard takes a tap. Android stops apps reading the clipboard in the background. PC to phone is automatic.

RCS chats stay on the phone. Apps can't read them. You can still reply to their notifications from the PC.

No iPhone. iOS blocks texts, calls and other apps' notifications.

Windows' rules

Call audio stays on your phone. Windows keeps Bluetooth call audio for its own apps, so you answer on the PC and talk on the phone.

One phone connected at a time. Pair as many as you like and switch in Settings.

Pair on the same network. After that, a VPN address works from anywhere.

Set up in about a minute.

  1. Install on the PC

    It lives in the tray, out of your way.

  2. Scan with the phone

    Open the app, scan the QR code on the PC.

  3. Done

    It reconnects on its own whenever both are on.

Questions

Is Palwyn free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. No paid plans, no ads, no account.

Does it send my data to the cloud?

No. The phone and PC talk directly over your own network with encrypted, pinned TLS 1.3. There is no Palwyn server.

Which phones and PCs does it work with?

Android 10 or newer, and Windows 10 (version 2004) or newer. iPhone isn't supported.

Can I talk on calls through my PC?

No. You see who's calling and answer, decline or end calls from the PC, but the audio stays on the phone. Windows keeps Bluetooth call audio for its own apps.

Is it an alternative to Phone Link?

Yes, for Android phones. It covers calls, messages, notifications, photos and files, and adds a touchpad remote, your phone's screen on the PC and file transfer both ways, all without an account.

Does it work away from home?

Pairing needs both on the same network. After that, give the PC your phone's VPN address (Tailscale, ZeroTier) and it connects there.
